DOCUMENTATION / DOMAINS

Domains &
networking

Make services reachable and understand network restrictions.

LOCAL ACCESS

Test on your device first

Start with a service bound to localhost and confirm it responds from the Android device.

LAN ACCESS

Check local network routing

Access from another device may require binding to the LAN interface and allowing traffic through your router or firewall.

PUBLIC ACCESS

Carrier networks vary

Mobile carriers may use CGNAT or block inbound connections. A custom domain alone does not make a private device reachable from the internet.

DNS

Point records to a reachable endpoint

Configure DNS only after you have a stable public endpoint or tunnel. Keep DNS records and TLS configuration aligned.

CUSTOM DOMAINS

Plan support

Custom domain support is included in the Free plan description; actual public routing depends on your network setup.
